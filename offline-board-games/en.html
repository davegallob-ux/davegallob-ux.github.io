Offline Board Games - Support

Welcome to the support page for Offline Board Games! Play Chess, Ludo, Sea Battle, and Checkers with your friends – completely without an internet connection.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Do I need an internet connection?
No. The app is specifically designed to allow games over local networks (WiFi) or via Bluetooth/WiFi Direct (Nearby Connections) without requiring an active internet connection.

2. Which games are available?
Currently, you can play Chess, Ludo, Sea Battle and Checkers.

3. How do I connect with another player?
One player must "host" a game, while the other "searches" for available games. Make sure you are both either on the same WiFi network or have Bluetooth and location services enabled if you are using Nearby Connections.

4. Why does the app need location access (Android only)?
On Android devices, location permission is required to find other devices via Bluetooth or WiFi Direct nearby. We do not collect or store any location data.

5. How do I remove ads?
In the Settings, you can choose "Get Premium." This removes ads and permanently unlocks additional features like hosting games.
