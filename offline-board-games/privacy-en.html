Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

David Gallob ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Offline Board Games mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from you. The app is designed to function primarily offline.

2. Third-Party Services

Our Service uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (like Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA) to show personalized or non-personalized ads. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services / Apple App Store: Used for processing In-App Purchases. Payment information is handled exclusively by the respective store providers.

3. Permissions and Local Connectivity

The Service requires certain permissions to enable multiplayer features:

  • Local Network & Bluetooth: Used to connect with other players nearby. No data transmitted via these connections is sent to us or any third party.
  • Location (Android only): Required by the Android operating system to discover nearby Bluetooth/WiFi Direct devices. We do not track, store, or share your physical location.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
